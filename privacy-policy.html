Information We Collect

When you browse HM TRADELINK or place an order, we collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, shipping address, and payment details, along with basic technical data like browser type and device information to keep the site running smoothly.

We use your information to process orders, communicate about bespoke commissions, provide customer support, and improve our catalog and services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We use minimal, functional cookies and local storage to remember items in your cart between visits. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
