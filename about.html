Thirty Years of Unyielding Legacy.

Born from a specialized machine shop in the industrial heartland, HM TRADELINK began with a single objective: to solve the tolerance challenges that others deemed impossible. For over three decades, we have remained at the intersection of metallurgy and craftsmanship.

"Precision is not just a metric; it's a philosophy," says Harshit Mandowra, our founder. This belief drives our commitment to utilizing exclusively medical-grade 316L stainless steel. We understand that in critical industrial applications, there is no margin for error.
